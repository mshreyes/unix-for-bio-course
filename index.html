Unix for Biology

A hands-on, practical course on harnessing the Unix shell for biological data. Learn how to inspect FASTA/FASTQ files, filter genomic tables (BED/VCF/GFF), parse annotations with awk and sed, and build reproducible bioinformatics one-liners. Modules 1 to 6 focus on core Unix tools; modules 7 and 8 introduce scalable workflows, cluster computing, and reproducible pipelines.

Live Biological Unix Sandbox

In-browser pipeline emulator. Supports Unix pipes (|), simple quotes, and foundational arguments for quick biological prototyping.
